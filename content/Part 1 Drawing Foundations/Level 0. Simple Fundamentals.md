<p class="lesson-kicker">Part 1 - Drawing foundations</p>

This is the most important stage. If you feel very lost, this teaches you how to use your eyes and hands.

My personal picks for the fundamental of construction, would be copying what you see on **Peter Hans Dynamic Bible** as many times as you want. Make it into a daily exercise if you feel like this is one of your main weaknesses. Grind it daily for months beside other tasks.
