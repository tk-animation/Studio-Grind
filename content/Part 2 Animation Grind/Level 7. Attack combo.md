<p class="lesson-kicker">Part 2 - Animation practice</p>

Animate a short attack combo for your character, making sure you analyze the intention, body-mechanics, ways to output energy and force through attacks, weight-transfer mechanics and so on. Take it into your own hands, the internet has infinite resources. Your animation will turn out good as long as you can reach your desired intention with your drawings. Analyze your own decisions with every part of the body so your attacks are able to demonstrate the most power and are highly based on human body realistic mechanics of movement.
