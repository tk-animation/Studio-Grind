<p class="lesson-kicker">Part 2 - Animation practice</p>

Let's all grind through exercises. Pick a level for you and let's go up forever. We criticize each other and help each other go up. 

First one will be: 

Ball

Animate whatever you want with a ball. If you feel like a very beginner, do your bouncing ball, try different weights. 
If you feel like doing it even though you are more advanced, explore different things, use your creativity, you can do whatever you want.

Challenge yourself, surprise yourself.
