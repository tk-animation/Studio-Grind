<p class="lesson-kicker">Part 2 - Animation practice</p>

Here we dive into effects animation for some time, and try to fill our skill-sheet with these fundamentals. Effects will be a very useful skill to grind, and since we will be always trying to get better at it, I decided to put it in between the lessons, so you can do it while doing the others. 

We start with the exploration of simple explosions and how they work (if this feels to simple, draw large-scale complex explosions);

Then we challenge ourselves into understanding a fire-loop;

And we get creative with water animation (challenge yourself based on your desire);


Inoue resource on fire effects: https://pa-works.jp/howto/ef_fires.html
and water: [https://pa-works.jp/howto/ef_water.html](https://pa-works.jp/howto/ef_water.html "https://pa-works.jp/howto/ef_water.html")
