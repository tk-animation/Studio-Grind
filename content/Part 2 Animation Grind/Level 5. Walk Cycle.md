<p class="lesson-kicker">Part 2 - Animation practice</p>

I feel like this is where we start to go into advanced territory. A walk cycle is the way you benchmark your ability to keep it simple but make it beautiful. 

Animate a walk cycle, you can try with different characters if you want a challenge, try to give them personality. Each person walks differently, and this is a very hard and specific thing to observe and its insanely beautiful when you train your ability to translate personality in your character's actions.

You can and probably SHOULD try a run cycle too, since we are getting into character acting territory, there's no such thing as "too much training".

Toshiyuki Inoue cool resource on it: https://pa-works.jp/howto/aruki.html
