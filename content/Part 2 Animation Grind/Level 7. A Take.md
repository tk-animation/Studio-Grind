<p class="lesson-kicker">Part 2 - Animation practice</p>

Animate a movement that startles a reaction. It's a fun exercise and very useful in visual storytelling.

In cartoon, we usually think of big and exaggerated reactions like these:
![[655387d15d413a08b226b3cf_617cb16be1152df1fcd6505a_animating-character-takes-1.gif|438]]

But it can of course be subtle, like 
![[655387d15d413a08b226b3c6_617cb18515bcde2f5bb8f36d_animating-character-takes-2.gif|466]]

Or like these, in anime![[655387d15d413a08b226b3fa_617cb221067fa8dd9f583741_ezgif-2-e763163eb4aa.gif|458]]

The 4 Basic Poses are:

- Start
- Anticipation
- Accent (when it "Pops" out)
- Settle (less exaggerated version of accent pose).

Read more on: https://www.schoolofmotion.com/blog/how-to-animate-character-takes !

One Piece has many of these.
