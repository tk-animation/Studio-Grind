<p class="lesson-kicker">Part 2 - Animation practice</p>

To help us master volume  control, spatial awareness, perspective, model control, inbetweening and more, we will draw:

Difficulty 1: A simple pose, waist up (or even shoulders-up ) and rotate it
Difficulty 2: Full body pose
Difficulty 3: Dynamic pose
Difficulty 4: Rotation a pose while it moves.

in 8 drawings.
