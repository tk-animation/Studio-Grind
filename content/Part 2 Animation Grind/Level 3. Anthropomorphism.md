<p class="lesson-kicker">Part 2 - Animation practice</p>

Pick an object, make it move, twist, bend, perform, mimic human actions.  Apply human attributes to it. Imagine it has a spine. Imagine its thinking. It's a great exercise for squash and stretch. 

Suggestion for objects: Book, eraser, bottle, phone, pencil, scissors, cup, flower, knife, spoon, hat, leaf, guitar, cactus, handbag, cheese, bread, hammer, log, scarf, gum, sock, trousers, cape, towel, bin... Infinite possibilities.
